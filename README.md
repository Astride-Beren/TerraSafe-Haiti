About the Project

TerraSafe Haiti is a research project exploring how artificial intelligence and environmental data can help identify areas at risk of landslides in Haiti.

The project focuses on the Tiburon Peninsula in southern Haiti, following the magnitude 7.2 earthquake of August 14, 2021, and the heavy rainfall associated with Tropical Storm Grace.

The Problem

Landslides can threaten lives, homes, roads, and essential infrastructure. In Haiti, earthquakes, intense rainfall, steep terrain, and environmental degradation can contribute to landslide hazards.

Identifying areas that may be more susceptible to landslides could help communities and decision-makers improve disaster preparedness.

Our Approach

The project aims to explore the use of machine learning alongside environmental and geospatial data, including:

- Earthquake shaking intensity
- Rainfall measurements
- Slope and elevation
- Vegetation indicators
- Historical landslide locations

These variables will be investigated to assess whether they can help distinguish locations with different levels of landslide susceptibility.

Current Status

TerraSafe Haiti is currently in the research and development stage.

Data collection, processing, model development, and evaluation are still in progress. No validated predictive results are being claimed at this stage.

Long-Term Vision

Our goal is to explore an accessible, data-driven approach that could eventually support landslide risk assessment and disaster preparedness in Haiti.
